- Docker Swarm deployment behind Traefik, with monitoring (metrics, centralized logs) and
  automatic backups
- **Dedicated image server** for champion portraits, resized automatically
- **ELO and tier**: inline editing by officers, end-of-war dialog with a win/loss toggle and ELO
  entry depending on the season, alliance statistics with ranking and season score per player
- **Google login** alongside Discord, with an identifier generated at sign-up
- Fight completion, same champion allowed on several defense nodes, editing your own identifier
- Prefight nodes highlighted on the war map

### Fixed

- Rate limiting counted requests per reverse proxy IP rather than per client, capping everyone at
  once; it is disabled until reading the `X-Forwarded-For` header is made reliable

## [0.3.0] — 2026-04-19

### Added

- **Attack synergies**: candidate search, same champion refused as a provider
- **War bans**: up to 6 banned champions, with alias suggestions in the defense view
- **Prefight**: selector grouped by player, badge and portrait in the attacker row, enabled per
  champion on the admin side, one champion usable on several nodes
- **Seasons**: admin management, banner on the war page, war tied to the active season at creation
- **Masteries**: administrable catalog and entry per game account
- KO counter, defender replacement with confirmation, preferred attacker carried by the war
  placement
- Shared filters by class, saga, player and preference in the selectors

## [0.2.0] — 2026-03-31

### Added

- **Alliance war**: war map, placements, attackers panel
- **Backups**: scheduled dump, local and remote purge, manual restore
- Champion and user administration, promotion and demotion
- Defense import and export, automatic display refresh
- Forced HTTPS, hardened headers, login rate limiting, secure cookies
- Mobile settings for language and theme
