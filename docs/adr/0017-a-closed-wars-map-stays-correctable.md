# A closed War's map stays correctable

Closing a War is final for what the War itself carries — Opponent, War Bans, result, Elo —
because a whole War is there to get those right. Its map is not: a defender, an Attacker,
a boost or a Synergy Carrier changed at the last minute is a slip found only afterwards.
So a Strategist or above keeps the rights of a running War on a closed one, for as long as
its Season is the Latest Season. Plain members and Visitors read it only.

When the Attacker is corrected, its Fight Record is re-frozen from the new Attacker's
current rank and ascension, not its rank at the time — which nothing recorded. We accept
the lie because it only ever points one way: ranks go up, so a corrected fight can read
easier than it was, never harder.

## Consequences

A node whose Attacker belongs to a Player no longer in that Battlegroup — moved or gone —
is locked on a closed War: its Attacker can be neither changed nor removed, and its
defender neither replaced nor removed, since that tears the Attacker down. The rest of the
node stays correctable. This protects a departed Player's history and roster. A running War
has no such lock: moving someone who changed group mid-war is ordinary play.

A closed War is corrected under its own Season's format, never the format of the Season
being prepared. Off-season Wars have no Latest Season to belong to and are sealed at close.
